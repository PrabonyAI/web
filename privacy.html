7 Microphone Access & Audio Data Usage

Our application may request access to your device’s microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) to enable voice-based features such as voice input, speech-to-text functionality, or interactive voice commands.

When you use these features:

  • Audio is recorded only when you explicitly activate voice input within the app.
  • The recorded audio may be processed in real-time to convert speech into text or to generate responses.
  • We do not continuously record audio in the background.
  • We do not store your audio recordings on our servers unless explicitly required for providing the requested service, and only with your consent.
  • Audio data may be transmitted securely to third-party service providers (such as AI or speech-processing APIs) solely for processing and delivering the requested functionality.

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data during transmission and processing.

By using voice-enabled features, you consent to the collection and use of audio data as described in this section.
